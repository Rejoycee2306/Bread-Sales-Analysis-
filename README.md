# Fortunate Bread Sales Analysis

My first data analytics project, built in Excel to practise the fundamentals.

## Overview:

‎This project analyzes Fortunate Bread Sales data to understand sales trend and product performance across four bread sizes, states and months. 

## Tools Used:

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Analysis Toolpak 

## Process:

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Descriptive statistical analysis
- Pivot tables
- Charts and dashboard 

## Key insights:

- **Stable overall performance:** total revenue was 18.5 million, with an average spend of 1,855 per transaction.

- **Volumes were even, revenue was not:** each bread size sold a similar quantity (2400 to 2500 units), but **Extra-large bread recorded the highest share of sales (48%)**, indicating customer preference for higher-priced products (Large and Extra-Large). 

- **Strong growth in some states:** Nasarawa and Delta performed strongly, while several towns underperformed. 

- **Seasonal peaks:** Sales peaked during mid-year and festive months( July-September and December), reaching over 1.6 million, after a drop in February.

## Recommendations:

1. **Keep premium products available at peak times:** improve production planning and marketing so Lareg and Extra Large bread stay in stock during July to September and December.
  
2. **Target underperforming regions:** run strategic promotional campaigns in towns with weak sales.
   
3. **Lift low-sales periods:** use promotions to improve sales in low months such as February.

## Conclusion:

‎This analysis shows clear demand patterns that can help optimize sales, production and inventory planning.
